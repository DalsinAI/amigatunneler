SDL Tunneler: licences
======================

The game: SDL Tunneler by Taneli Kalvas (2003-2008), after Geoffrey
Silverton's Tunneler (1991), from https://github.com/guyromm/tunneler.

- The program is under the GNU General Public License, version 2 or later
  (COPYING.txt). It has no data files: its pictures are drawn by the
  program.
- sdl12-compat is linked into the program: Licences/sdl12-compat.txt.
- The Amiga port's own files (amiga/, AMIGA_PORT.md, package/, tools/,
  tests/) are GPL 2 or later, like the game; the GlowIcon art is The
  AmiCradle Team's own.
